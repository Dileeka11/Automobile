How It Works

The Importation Roadmap

We make direct importing easy and transparent with five structured, tracked milestones.

01

Inquiry & Model Selection

Submit your preferred specifications. We select matching vehicles directly from premier Japanese and European auctions.

02

Quotation & Agreement

Receive a comprehensive CIF cost breakdown. Lock in pricing with a secure digital import agreement.

03

Logistics & Shipping

We handle export permits, JAAI inspections, shipping bookings, and provide real-time vessel tracking.

04

Customs Clearance

Our dedicated port agents manage custom declarations (CUSDEC), tax evaluation, and bank releases.

05

DMT Registration & Delivery

We process the RMV registration plate, complete detailed quality checks, and deliver your vehicle key.
